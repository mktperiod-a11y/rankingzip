Summer 2026 Anime Rankings – Week 12

Written by Zeerak September 25, 2026

The twelfth and final weekly poll for the Summer 2026 anime season has concluded, with Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation Season 3 ending the season with its third consecutive win.

Mushoku Tensei took first place with 16.97% of the vote, its highest percentage of the season. Re:ZERO -Starting Life in Another World- Season 4 was just behind with 16.80%, leaving only 0.17 percentage points between the top two.

Love Unseen Beneath the Clear Night Sky made one of the biggest jumps of the week, moving from 12th to third with 6.26%. Though I Am an Inept Villainess followed in fourth, while Saga of Tanya the Evil II completed the top five.

As always, these rankings reflect the preferences of the Anime Corner community and are not intended to determine what anyone should or should not watch. Every anime has its own appeal, and the final ranking is simply a look at which episodes our voters enjoyed most this week.

Top 59 Anime of the Week 12 – Summer 2026

Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation Season 3 closes out the weekly polls with three straight wins, having taken first place in Weeks 10, 11, and 12. Its 16.97% result this week is also its highest vote percentage of the season.

The final race for first was also one of the closest of the season, with only 0.17 percentage points separating Mushoku Tensei and Re:ZERO. Further down, Love Unseen Beneath the Clear Night Sky returned to the top ten in a big way, jumping from 12th to third.

You can view the full Week 12 results, including additional voting insights and demographic breakdowns, on the Anime Corner Polls platform. With the weekly polls now concluded, you can also vote in our Summer 2026 Anime of the Season polls until October 5 and explore results from previous weeks at animecorner.me/polls.
