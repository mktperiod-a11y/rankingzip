'''Men's [[baseball]]''' at the '''[[2026 Asian Games]]''' is being held between 21 and 27 September 2026 at two locations in [[Aichi Prefecture]]. Featuring 8 teams in two groups, it marked the first baseball event at the Asian Games with an one-stage preliminary round since [[Baseball at the 2014 Asian Games|2014]].
