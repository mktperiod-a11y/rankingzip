'''Men's [[baseball]]''' made its first appearance as a demonstration sport at the 1990. Baseball became a full medal sport at the [[1994 Asian Games]] in [[Hiroshima]], [[Japan]], and has been a sport in the games since then.<ref>{{cite web |title=History of Asian Games |url=https://www.insidethegames.biz/articles/1059784/history-of-asian-games |website=insidethegames.biz |access-date=1 November 2024}}</ref> International baseball in Asia is governed by the [[World Baseball Softball Confederation]] (WBSC) and the [[Baseball Federation of Asia]] (BFA).

The competition has been dominated by teams from Japan, South Korea and Chinese Taipei (Taiwan). South Korea is the most successful team by winning 6 gold medals.
